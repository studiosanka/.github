# Getting help

1. **Read the build guide.** It is in the `docs` folder of the [sixeyes repository](https://github.com/studiosanka/sixeyes/tree/main/docs). It says what is finished and what is not. (It will also be on our website, which is coming soon.)
2. **Ask in an issue** in the repository your question is about. Pick the **Question** form (or **Build problem** if you are stuck on a step). A photo helps a lot for hardware.
3. **For anything dangerous or security related,** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

We are a small group of volunteers. We read everything but cannot promise an answer to every message.
