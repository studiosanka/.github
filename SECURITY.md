# Safety and security

## If something is dangerous right now

Switch the arm off at the power supply and unplug it. If anything is hot, smoking or sparking, do that first and ask questions afterwards. The arm runs from a 24 V supply and should always have a fuse in the supply line (see the build guide).

## What to report privately

Please do **not** put these in a public issue:

- A way to make the arm move, or keep moving, when it should have stopped (for example a problem with a stop button or with the stop-on-lost-connection rule).
- A way for someone nearby to take control of an arm without pairing with it.
- A mistake in the circuit board design that could cause a fire or an electric shock.
- A security hole in the website or in the phone controller page.

How: on the repository, open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. *(Studio Sanka: switch on private vulnerability reporting in each repository's settings, or in the organisation settings, before publishing. Until a contact address is added, this is the only private route.)*

Say what you saw, how to repeat it, and what you were using. We will reply as soon as we can. We are a small group of volunteers and cannot promise a time.

## Everything else

Ordinary bugs, build problems and questions are welcome as normal [issues](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Versions

SixEyes is pre-release. There are no supported versions yet: fixes go into the newest code.
